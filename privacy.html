Privacy policy

Midweek Lights · last updated 9 November 2026

Midweek Lights is an unofficial, non-commercial Champions League fan app run by TheDigitalJourney. This page explains what happens to your data.

What we collect

  • When you sign in with Google: your Google account's email address and a unique account ID, used only to sign you in.
  • What you enter in the app: your manager name, team name, fantasy picks, captain, league codes and score predictions.

Browsing the table, matches, players and news needs no account, and we don't use analytics or advertising trackers.

To show live scores, stats and news, your browser loads public data directly from ESPN (site.api.espn.com). Like any website you visit, ESPN can see your IP address for those requests. No account details are sent to ESPN.

Who can see it

Other signed-in players can see your manager name, team name, picks and predictions on league leaderboards. Your email address is never shown to other players.

Where it is stored

Account and fantasy data is stored with Supabase (EU, London region). The website itself is hosted on GitHub Pages. We don't sell or share your data with anyone else.

Deleting your data

To delete your account and all fantasy data, email the address on the app's Google sign-in screen and we'll remove it within 30 days.

Not affiliated

Midweek Lights is not affiliated with UEFA or any club. Match information comes from public sources listed in the app.
